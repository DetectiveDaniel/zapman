# SPEEDING PLANES!

A fast cockpit arcade game where you fly a plane through three missions, lock targets, fire guns, launch missiles, and try to survive before damage reaches 100%.

The main menu lets you pick a plane and missile loadout before launch:

- P-51 Mustang: usual loadout, 320 gunpower and 3 missiles
- Hawker Hurricane: 185 gunpower and fires two smaller missiles at once
- Supermarine Spitfire: 190 gunpower and fires powerful bombs that set planes on fire
- Messerschmitt Bf 109: 200 gunpower and 5 missiles
- Mitsubishi A6M Zero: 420 gunpower and laser fire
- Speed missiles: faster and better at targeting
- Explosion missiles: slower but create a huge blast

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls

- `WASD` or arrow keys: fly
- `V`: lock nearest target
- `Space`: fire machine guns
- `G`: fire targeted missile
- `F5`: switch between cockpit view and chase-plane view
- `A` / `D`: spin the plane while in chase-plane view

Damage starts at `0%`. At `100%`, the plane goes down.
