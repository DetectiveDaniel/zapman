# Snake Clash

A smooth browser arena game where a green snake hunts colorful rival snakes, grows one circle at a time, and transforms into a short armored dragon at 36 circles.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

- Steer with WASD, arrow keys, mouse, or touch.
- Hold Space to boost. On touch screens, double-tap and hold.
- Bite rival snakes to steal circles and grow.
- Reach 36 circles to awaken the dragon.
