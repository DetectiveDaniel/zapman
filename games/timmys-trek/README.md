# Timmy's Trek

A pixel-style runner where Timmy chooses a world, dashes across three levels, grabs coins, stomps enemies into smoke, and enters portals.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Worlds

- Grassy World: original grass, slugs, bees, springs, and throwers
- Lava World: ladders, extra platforms, and lava lakes
- Beach World: sand, ocean, bees, and slugs
- City World: traffic, cars that squish you, and people who throw objects

## Shop

Collect coins and use the Shop button to buy powers for 15 coins each.

- Zing: glide with wings while holding jump
- Penalty: throw a returning football
- Griddle: throw fireballs
- Liver: add 3 extra hearts
- Lava Walker: walk on lava safely
- Home: ride a hoverboard and float above danger
- Adventure: move really fast and explode things by touching them
- Jumpy: jump really high
- Liver 2: toggle 12 hearts
- Fan: turn into a fan and blow things away

## Controls

- Move: `A` / `D` or arrow keys
- Jump: `W`, up arrow, or space
- Dash: `Shift` or `X`
- Laser: `F` or `Control`
- Touch buttons are available on screen
