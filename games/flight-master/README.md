# Flight Master

Flight Master is a fast arcade canvas game. You fly a jet, fire blue lasers, shoot enemy planes, dodge helicopter bombs, and avoid buildings. Damage starts at 0 and rises based on how serious each hit is.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls

- Move with `WASD` or arrow keys.
- Shoot with `Space` or by clicking the game.
- Restart with `R` or the Restart button.

## Scoring

- Enemy plane: 55 points
- Helicopter: 90 points
- Dodged building: 10 points
- Red dot hit: 10 damage
- Bomb hit: 14 damage
- Helicopter crash: 18 damage
- Plane collision: 24 damage
- Building strike: instant death and explosion
