# PARKOURZ!

A pixel parkour browser game with jumping, running, kicking, lucky blocks, green tunnels, random powers, circling enemies, five hearts, and a glowing yellow win message at the flag.
Reach the first flag to unlock Level 2, where spiky metal boxes move up and down.
Reach the second flag to unlock Level 3, a lava land with skeleton humans, moving platforms, and lava below you.
Reach the third flag to unlock Level 4, Tank Trouble, where evil tanks split into bigger bosses with boss health bars.
Reach the fourth flag to unlock Level 5, where people throw fireballs and random potions that either slow you down or make you huge.
Reach the fifth flag to unlock Level 6, a rainy final challenge packed with the things you faced before, including moving platforms, spiky boxes, skeletons, mummies, future skeletons, casters, tanks, pipes, a huge gorilla boss, and a rescue at the top.
After the gorilla stage comes another level where a rival player fights you with 5 hearts, uses powers, and grows bigger every 10 seconds.

The main menu also includes Moving Mayhem and Make Your Own PARKOURZ, where you can place blocks, lucky blocks, spiky boxes, moving platforms, skeletons, big bosses, tanks, a start spot, and a flag.
Me Invaders lets you choose an enemy and try to stop the player from reaching the flag.
SURVIVAL sends endless waves of attackers until you run out of hearts.
Tanky Town lets you drive a tank through a city, and if you destroy a town tank the police tanks start hunting you.
Arena lets you choose buttons for things you have fought, place infinite fighters on the arena floor, and press `Fight` to make them battle each other. Arena fighters use their own powers, like missiles, shurikens, potions, lasers, and evoker fangs.
Arena placement now lets you click anywhere in the arena to drop fighters in different places.
After the rival fight, a parade celebration can start in Tanky Town, and the shops there let you buy or toggle a flashlight for FOGAPOCO and a hoverboard that lets you fly.
The parade now has a `MOVE ON` button that starts another story level with lots of ally players and big grabber enemies that can throw you into the air for 2 hearts of damage when you crash back down.
After that, a dungeon guide asks for help finding three magic power crystals. The screen fades black, you teleport into three ninja crystal levels with one crystal in each, and you can throw shurikens with `F`; shurikens defeat anything in 3 hits while enemy ninjas use float, tiny, one-heart, and falling fist attacks.
When all three crystal levels are complete, the guide returns for a farewell and gives you peanut Chews plus the wrapper before a slimmer rival battle begins.
After the slim rival, a potion giant gauntlet opens with potion throwers, lucky blocks, and huge grabber enemies.
After that, a rainy challenger level begins with three challenger rivals and lots of lucky-block powerups.
FOGAPOCO is a foggy mini game where the projectiles are the clearest things you can see.
TIME TWIST: THE ESCAPE sends you through Ancient Egypt with slow mummies and then into the future with laser skeletons that recharge every 3 seconds.
Night of the Ninjas fills a dark level with loads of ninjas, different ninja abilities, and lucky blocks everywhere.
After Night of the Ninjas, a dungeon evoker level appears where grounded evokers summon fang attacks that take one heart.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls

- `A` / `D` or arrow keys: walk and run
- `W`, `Up`, or `Space`: jump
- `S` / `Down`: crawl, or enter a green tunnel while standing on it
- `S` / `Down` plus jump: crouch-jump extra high
- `K`: kick
- `F`: throw the current power
- `E`: use Tanky Town shops
- `Skip Level`: jump to the next level
- `Restart`: start over

## Lucky Block Powers

- Lightning bolt: grow big for a while
- Fireball: defeat enemies
- Ice cube: freeze enemies for 10 seconds
- Laser: zap enemies
