# Zapman

A self-contained pixel adventure. Open `index.html`, or choose **Zapman**, pinned first in the main picker. No server, installation or downloads are required.

## Story

Find a strange watch, put it in your bag, and press its button. Walk into the portal and meet Sapphire. She asks you to save your dimension. **YES** starts the fading Zapman Studios logo/title sequence, then reveals the city. **Not yet** lets you stay and decide later.

The portal chooses a different colour from its last opening, including across visits when browser storage is available. All artwork is original code/vector pixel artwork inspired by the supplied watch, traveller, Sapphire, portal and lightning references.

## Controls

- **Arrows / WASD:** move. Diagonal movement has the same speed as straight movement.
- **E / Enter on the canvas:** continue the watch story or say **Okay** to Sapphire at home. The on-screen buttons work with keyboard, mouse and touch.
- **C / Shrink button:** shrink or grow. Tiny travellers are not detected by city robots, which stop aiming/firing at them. Existing beams and touching robots still hurt at either size. In Level 2, distant people-robots overlook a tiny traveller, but nearby machines and scorpions still hurt. You cannot grow inside an obstacle. In Level 9, your watch is jammed: trying to shrink reveals the problem, and powers remain unavailable.
- **J / Space / click the canvas / WATCH ZAP in Levels 5–7:** fire a watch bolt at the nearest living Guardian, volcanic robot or Roman soldier within range. Hold J, Space or the touch button to keep firing. Cover blocks bolts and enemy rays alike.
- **Skip Level:** advance the active level, including after game over or while paused. Home and vial-handover scenes still play. In Level 5 it skips the corridor to the computer's speech, the arena to the explosion, or the portal exit to Level 6. Level 6 leads to The Hysterics; skipping Level 7's fight still plays the innocence/vial story, and skipping its exit opens the portal to The Sea of Memories. Level 8 leads to Powerless; skipping Level 9 returns to Sapphire and preserves the watch-repair story. After the repair, Sapphire sends you to Level 10. Skipping Level 10 preserves her warning about the darkness. It is disabled during story scenes, entrance titles and after victory.
- **P / Escape / Pause:** pause any level or either portal walk. Hidden tabs freeze the adventure, including temporary thought effects; leaving the window releases held movement keys.
- **R after defeat / Retry:** restart the current level with five hearts. Losing in later levels does not send you back through the city.
- **Restart story:** replay the full adventure, including a fresh portal colour when opened.

## Level 1: The City — Dimension 0012

The first robot starts over 700 pixels away from the arrival point. Seven robots patrol the city; normal-size travellers within detection range attract laser fire after a warning. Planters stop beams and movement. There is a short period of protection after a hit. Follow the street to the chequered line at the far right to enter Level 2. The shrinking ability has no time limit.

## Level 2: The Rip in the Wardrobe — Dimension 0001, Earth

You arrive inside your house. A coloured portal tears open inside the wardrobe and robots emerge. You run outside, then watch them break down the front door in a short animation. Both scenes support Pause. The town starts safely away from the first robot, with five hearts and brief protection.

Thirty people wander through town. Five invading robots launch scorpions at nearby humans. The scorpions fly out of the launchers, then crawl after unconverted people; people flee nearby scorpions. A caught person becomes a metal-clothed people-robot with a glowing red eye and begins chasing the traveller. A scorpion catching the traveller immediately transforms you into a people-robot: **GAME OVER**, at either size and even during ordinary hit protection. The defeat screen shows your transformed character. Invading robots and people-robots cost one heart on contact, with brief protection between hits. Tiny travellers escape distant people-robots' attention, but close machines still hurt. Planters block movement.

Reach the green **antidote** outside the clinic at the far end of town. Picking it up removes the scorpions and turns every people-robot back into a human. After a short cure animation, the story continues to Level 3, Rise of the Practiri. Retry restarts Level 2's house and door sequence with everyone human; Skip Level advances to Level 3. Pause and hidden tabs freeze the introduction, scorpions, crowd and cure animation.

## Level 3: Rise of the Practiri — Dimension 3604

Cross a grassy field with alternating stone walls. Six hungry Practiri hunt nearby people and the traveller, taking detours around walls. Wandering people flee; if caught, they disappear with a cartoon **GULP!** animation. Practiri bites cost the traveller one heart, with brief protection afterwards. Shrinking hides you from distant Practiri, but nearby lizards can still notice and bite you. The arrival area is clear of enemies.

Weave through the wall openings to the house at the far end. You arrive home safely, and a fresh portal opens beside the house. Sapphire steps out and says: “You need to find a thought vial at the end of the next dimension.” Choose **Okay** (or E), see the traveller's reply, then walk into the portal for Level 4.

## Level 4: The Thought Dimension — Dimension 0984

A thought machine stands in the middle of the dimension. Walk around its solid base and use the metal barriers for cover. Its aimed lasers cause one of four random effects on a hit: random walking, reversed controls, dancing in a circle, or freezing in place. Each effect lasts **two seconds**, followed by **one second** of protection from further effects. Movement respects walls even when forced, and control always returns automatically. These rays scramble movement rather than taking hearts; shrinking does not hide you from the machine.

The HUD names the active effect and counts down. Pause and hidden tabs freeze effects and lasers. Collect the thought vial on its plinth at the far end and return to Sapphire. Choose **Give Sapphire the thought vial**. She says, “Aha! I have found your next dimension! It is Dimension 3564, The Contestants!” Choose **Enter Dimension 3564** to continue.

## Level 5: Dimension 3564, The Contestants

Five MBS Guardians carry tablets and fire pink happiness rays. A hit makes the traveller overjoyed and very quick for four seconds, while draining one heart every two seconds. Hits refresh the duration without resetting accumulated drain. Both sizes are affected. The HUD shows the speed effect, remaining time and falling hearts. Watch bolts defeat a corridor Guardian in three hits. Defeat all five and approach the computer at the far end.

The computer says, “Mwah ha ha! I evolved! You will do nicely for my team of Guardians!” Choose **NO! I will not surrender.** The traveller shouts the reply; a door opens and four more Guardians arrive with probes and tablets. The arena starts with five hearts, after a brief safe door animation. A purple conversion beam or touching an arena Guardian transforms the traveller into an MBS Guardian immediately: **GAME OVER**, even when tiny or under ordinary damage protection. Retry restarts this arena rather than the corridor. Arena Guardians take two watch hits each.

Defeat every arena Guardian and the computer cries “Noooooo!” and explodes. A thought portal opens, with a second thought vial on the plinth nearby. Collect the vial before entering the portal to reach Dimension 6493. No conversion rays remain after the last Guardian falls. Restart and retry clear old rays, watch bolts, happiness drain, conversion and ending state.

## Level 6: Out of the Flames — Dimension 6493

The thought portal leads inside a volcano. Cross an ash-and-basalt path surrounded by flowing lava, with nine lava vents in fixed locations and five hostile robots. The arrival area is clear of vents and enemies. The vents share a six-second cycle with different offsets: 3.6 seconds cooling, one second of amber warning with a ring and **!**, then 1.4 seconds spurting. Only an erupting jet hurts; warning vents are harmless. A jet costs one heart at either size, with the usual brief damage protection. Walk around jets or wait for them to cool.

Robots patrol and aim lasers at normal-size travellers. Shrinking hides you from their aim, but existing lasers and robot contact still hurt. The watch defeats each robot in three hits, and basalt boulders block movement and beams. You can escape without defeating every robot. Reach the cool green portal at the far end to enter Level 7.

Pause and hidden tabs freeze the vent cycle, lava animation, robots and projectiles together. Retry restarts Dimension 6493 with five hearts, fresh robots and the original vent timing; earlier levels do not replay. Skip Level also works after losing here.

## Level 7: The Hysterics

Enter a Roman colosseum with overturned wine bottles and spilled wine on the sandy arena floor. Ten Roman soldiers shout, **“You are a villain! Get him!”** Choose **Fight the ten soldiers** or press E to start. The arrival point is clear of soldiers.

Use **J / Space / click / WATCH ZAP** to fight. Each soldier yields after two watch hits. They approach and give a 0.7-second red-ring warning before a short spear attack; dodge out of range before the strike. A spear costs one heart, with the usual brief hit protection. Both sizes can be targeted. Wine bottles are scenery. Pause and hidden tabs freeze combat and the story animations. Retry restarts the accusation with ten fresh soldiers and five hearts.

After all ten yield, they declare, **“You are proved of innocence. Let us give you a reward. The vial!”** Choose **Take the vial**. You say **“Thanks!”** and both you and the soldiers smile. Walk to the arch on the right to open your own coloured portal. The soldiers stop smiling and show confused **?** faces as you step through. After the portal animation, Level 8 begins. No dimension number has been assigned to The Hysterics.

## Level 8: The Sea of Memories

Your portal from the Roman colosseum opens underwater. Bubbles carry memories of the strange watch, home and Sapphire. Water reduces movement speed by **15%** at both normal and tiny sizes; diagonal movement remains normalized. Six octopuses glide back and forth on fixed, gently bobbing routes. Their eight waving tentacles and body cost one heart on contact, with brief protection between hits. Their patrol routes are independent of your position, and the arrival area is clear of octopuses.

Nearby octopuses aim visible ink blobs at you, firing every 3.2 seconds after an initial delay. A blob that hits darkens the game screen for **two seconds**, with a countdown; movement remains available. Ink itself costs no hearts, and a fresh hit restarts the two-second effect. You can dodge blobs or use coral-covered rocks to block them. Both sizes can be inked. Pause and hidden tabs freeze shots and the darkness timer. Retry, restart, death and victory clear remaining ink and darkness.

Go around the coral-covered rocks and octopuses, then enter the glowing portal to reach Level 9. Shrinking remains available, and tentacles can hurt either size. Pause and hidden tabs freeze octopuses, bubbles and water animation. Retry restores five hearts and the original octopus positions. No dimension number has been assigned to this level.

## Level 9: Powerless

The sea portal leads to Betterworld. The first time you press **C** or the Shrink button, the traveller says **“My watch is jammed!”** in a speech bubble. You remain normal size. Further attempts remind you that it is still jammed; watch bolts are unavailable too. Move at the usual walking speed and find another thought vial at the far end.

Five invisible barriers block alternating parts of the street. They are solid but never drawn. A brief shimmer around the traveller indicates contact; explore above or below each blockage to find a way around. Six hostile Betterworlders, drawn with copper hair and pale blue clothes based on the supplied reference, pursue you when nearby. They respect the barriers and cost one heart on contact, with brief protection and knockback to let you escape. The arrival area is clear of enemies.

Collect the thought vial to return safely to Sapphire. Choose **Give Sapphire the vial and watch**. She repairs the jammed mechanism in a short workbench animation, then shrinking and watch bolts work again in her courtyard. Use C, J/Space, click or the touch buttons to try your restored powers, then choose **Thanks, Sapphire! What next?** Sapphire sends you to Cries of the Parasings; choose **Enter the rainforest portal** and walk into it on the right. Returning clears the hazards and restores five hearts. The courtyard portal does not restart the prologue.

Pause and hidden tabs freeze the Betterworlders, jam speech, collision shimmer, return and repair animation. Retry restores five hearts, the starting positions and the first watch-jam discovery; Restart clears the level and repair state. No dimension number has been assigned to Powerless.

## Level 10: Cries of the Parasings

Sapphire sends you through a new portal after fixing the watch. The new dimension is a dense rainforest with mossy roots, ferns, hanging vines and six fast-flying eagles. Your fixed watch can shrink and grow you with C or the touch button. **Lasers are disabled in this level**, including keyboard, canvas clicks and held controls. You arrive at normal size with five hearts, safely away from the first eagle.

Eagles patrol rapidly through the canopy, then mark a normal-size traveller's position with an amber **DIVE!** ring for 0.8 seconds before swooping at 310 pixels per second. Their dive aims at the marked spot rather than following you, so keep moving to dodge. Shrinking prevents new dives, but birds and dives already in progress still hurt at either size. Touching an eagle costs one heart, with brief protection between hits. They can fly over roots and return to their patrol after a dive. You must walk around the roots.

Reach Sapphire at the far end. She appears through the portal and says, **“There is a terrible darkness... You must defeat it.”** Choose **Enter the Shadow Dimension** to continue to Level 11. Retry restarts Level 10 with a fixed watch and fresh eagle positions; it does not repeat Powerless or the repair. Pause and hidden tabs freeze patrols, dive markers and flight. No dimension number has been assigned to this level.

## Level 11: The Shadow Dimension

After Sapphire's warning in the rainforest, enter a fog-covered dimension. Twenty-four living shadows fill the map, including your own silhouette following from behind. They chase you at both sizes. The repaired watch still shrinks and grows you, but cannot fire lasers here. You start with five hearts, a safe gap from enemies and two seconds of arrival protection.

Thick drifting fog covers the entire scene, with a small clear area around you. Follow the glowing lights along the central path. Each of three lights restores up to three hearts once and pushes nearby shadows away. Touching a shadow costs one heart, with brief hit protection and knockback to let you escape. Keep moving and dodge around approaching silhouettes to reach the far portal.

The exit clears the shadows and shows **Out of the shadows!** Retry rebuilds Level 11 with five hearts, unused lights and the repaired watch. Pause, hidden tabs and game over freeze the chase and fog. Skip Level reaches this ending; restart clears the shadows and lights. No dimension number has been assigned to this level.

## Friendly followers

Twelve wandering people populate the Practiri field, and eight populate Dimension 3564. Nearby people join the traveller and follow in a loose group, with a little extra speed to catch up if they fall behind. They respect walls and use openings. Unrecruited people wander; people in the grassy field flee nearby Practiri and can still be swallowed. The group resets when retrying or leaving its level. Pause and hidden tabs freeze followers with the rest of the game.

The game includes the shared display settings, black-and-white theme, Mobile Mode and top-left **GO BACK** button. `model.js` contains the simulation; `game.js` draws it and connects controls.
