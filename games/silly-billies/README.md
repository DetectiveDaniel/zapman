# Silly Billies

A colorful two-level side-scrolling browser game about Billie, a laser-firing boy racing through Silly City. Level 2 ends with a grey Super Silly Blaster that fires one-hit shots. The game includes synthesized arcade sound effects and upbeat background music.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

- Run: `A` / `D` or arrow keys
- Jump: `W`, up arrow, or space
- Fire: `F` or `J`
- Touch controls appear on smaller screens

Billie begins with ten hearts. Dodge hazards, defeat running monsters and laser-shooting robots, collect stars, and reach the golden door.
Use the **SOUND ON/OFF** button or press `M` to mute the music and effects.
