# Silly Billies

A colorful three-level side-scrolling browser game about Billie, a laser-firing boy racing through Silly City. Level 2 ends with a grey Super Silly Blaster that fires one-hit shots. Level 3, **Under the Drain**, enters flooded brick tunnels: bubbles warn before turquoise underwater monsters rise from drain holes, bats swoop and waterproof robots shoot. Monsters sink back into their holes on a six-second cycle and cannot hit or be shot while hidden. Bats can be zapped during their dives. The blaster carries into the drain if collected in Level 2. The game includes synthesized arcade sound effects and a lower, mysterious tune for the drain.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

- Run: `A` / `D` or arrow keys
- Jump: `W`, up arrow, or space
- Fire: `F` or `J`
- Touch controls appear on smaller screens; the picker's Mobile Mode adds a shared four-arrow pad.

Billie begins with ten hearts. Dodge hazards, defeat running monsters and laser-shooting robots, collect stars, and reach the golden door.
Use the **SOUND ON/OFF** button or press `M` to mute the music and effects.
