# Kiff-o-Kart

A local browser kart racing game made from the supplied character drawings.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Choose a racer, click a place on the map, then confirm `START RACE?`.

- Forward: Up arrow or W
- Steer: Left/right arrows or A/D
- Brake/reverse: Down arrow or S
- Activate stopped power-up: Space
- Reset race: R

Lucky blocks spin the two circular power-up slots. The stopped item becomes ready, and Space activates it.

## Courses

Each map spot now has its own course style:

- The Ramps: sky track, 3D green ramps, and moving blocks.
- Kitty Castle: loop-the-loops and blue speed boosts with screen blur.
- Bouncy Bed: bouncy tunnel sections that throw you in random directions.
- Sandy Hollows: swinging wrecking balls.
- The Snake: slippery road with wall bounces.
- Boomsday Castle: lava pools and spikes.
- The Sandy Tunnels: green desert tunnels.
- Cactusville Mountains: walls you weave through.
- Spiky Rocks: cactus obstacles on the course.
