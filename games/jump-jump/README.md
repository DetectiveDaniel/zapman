# JUMP JUMP

A tiny browser game where you launch a pixel jumper from a slingshot, smash towers, and knock out every angry enemy.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

- Drag the jumper backward from the slingshot.
- Pick a human from the bottom of the screen.
- Release to launch.
- Fired humans recharge the slingshot immediately, but each human can only be used once per level.
- Hit towers to knock blocks around.
- Touch every enemy to clear the level.
- Defeated faces drop gems.
- Faces can vanish if they fall too hard.
- Some faces carry umbrellas or laser goggles.
- Use Tower of Fortune to flip cards for gems, score, and hats.
- Later levels add portals and fans that blow your jumper around.
- Level 7 adds yellow jetpack faces that fly away from danger.
- Later levels mix red, wood, stone, and iron blocks with different toughness.
- Use `R` to restart and `N` for the next level.

## Humans

- Bonk zaps the nearest angry face.
- Drill zings through blocks without bouncing away.
- Spin launches fast and breaks through three layers.
- Frozo freezes nearby blocks and enemies.

## Tower of Fortune

- Flip one of three cards each floor.
- Gem cards add gems.
- Hat cards unlock and equip cowboy hats or space helmets.
- Angry face cards stop the Tower run.
